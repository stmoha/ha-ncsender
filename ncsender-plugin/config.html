Find it in Home Assistant ↗
It's on the ncSender integration page in Home Assistant: press Configure (the gear), and it's shown at the top. With it, buttons talk to Home Assistant directly and work with the controller off or a job paused. Leave it empty to send comment lines through the controller instead.

One per line: Name, entity_id. Add , hold to make both buttons need a 1-second press, e.g. Controller power, switch.slb_ext, hold.
